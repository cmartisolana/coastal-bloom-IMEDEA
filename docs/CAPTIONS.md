# Figure captions

**Eddies (`figures/eddies`). Regional offshore redistribution of eddy occurrence, 2003–2020.** Benguela (left) and Gulf Stream (right). (a,b) Percentage of days on which each mapped location was inside the union of META4.0 effective eddy contours. The solid black contour marks the mean monthly all-eddy centroid distance; the dashed contour marks 60 km. (c,d) Monthly all-eddy centroid (thin blue), robust STL trends for all eddies (thick blue), cyclonic eddies (black) and anticyclonic eddies (green). Dashed polarity curves indicate OLS-HAC intervals containing zero. The straight dashed blue fit is the descriptive Theil–Sen slope of the all-eddy STL trend. Legend values are seasonal OLS slopes in km yr⁻¹ with matching 95% HAC intervals, not uncertainties for the STL/Theil–Sen estimator. (e,f) OLS trends in each ring's share of annual summed monthly occupied-area means, in percentage points per decade; whiskers are 95% HAC intervals. Significant bars are orange within 60 km and blue beyond; non-significant bars are grey. Vertical shading marks 0–30 km as low reliability, but these eddy rings remain included. Ring share trends use 18 annual values; centroid fits use 216 monthly values. Significance is unadjusted across rings.

**Currents and Ekman (`figures/currents_Ekman`). Offshore currents and redistribution of the positive Ekman-divergence proxy, 2003–2020.** Regions are as in the eddy figure. (a,b) Mean offshore component of GlobCurrent Ekman velocity at 15 m, cm s⁻¹; red indicates seaward flow. The dashed contour marks 60 km. (c,d) Seasonal OLS trends in offshore Ekman velocity (green) and total geostrophic-plus-Ekman velocity (dark grey), cm s⁻¹ decade⁻¹, using 216 monthly observations. (e,f) Annual OLS trends in ring shares of the area-weighted positive part of monthly ring-mean Ekman divergence, 30–200 km, percentage points per decade (18 annual values). The inner 30 km are excluded only from this share diagnostic. Whiskers are 95% HAC intervals; grey bars have intervals containing zero. Significant share bars are orange within 60 km and blue beyond. Nearshore aggregate annotations refer to 30–60 km shares. The shaded inner 30 km in velocity panels remain included but are least reliable. This local divergence proxy is not a measurement of vertical velocity, coastal upwelling transport or nutrient supply.

**Eddy polarity (`figures/eddy_polarity_share`). Eddy-occurrence contribution-share trends by polarity.** Benguela (a) and Gulf Stream (b). Trends in each 10 km ring's share of summed monthly occupied-area means, calculated separately and normalised within cyclonic (black) and anticyclonic (green) eddy coverage. Trends are percentage points per decade, estimated from 18 annual values. Whiskers show 95% HAC intervals; grey indicates intervals containing zero. The dashed line marks 60 km and shading marks the low-reliability 0–30 km strip, which is included. Tests are unadjusted for multiple comparisons and adjacent rings are not independent.
